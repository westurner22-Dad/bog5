# Bundled fonts
Cinzel, Cinzel Decorative (Natanael Gama), Alegreya, Alegreya Sans, Alegreya Sans SC (Huerta Tipográfica).
All are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org) and were copied from the
Google Fonts set installed on the build box, so the app renders identically offline on any phone.
